Mateo La Serna

Narrative & Documentary Filmmaker • Hollywood Production Assistant

Mateo La Serna is a Peruvian-born filmmaker raised in North Carolina.

His work explores identity, culture, and experiences that often go unheard. Through documentary and narrative filmmaking, Mateo creates work that challenges perspectives, preserves cultural memory, and brings audiences closer to worlds beyond their own.

Alongside directing, cinematography, and editing across short films and documentary series, Mateo brings hands-on Hollywood set experience as a professional Production Assistant—well-versed in fast-paced set operations, lockups, talent coordination, and high-stakes crew workflows.
